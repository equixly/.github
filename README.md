## Welcome to Equixly
Hi, and thanks for stopping by. 

Equixly is a continuous offensive security platform. We run autonomous penetration testing and AI red teaming to validate whether security vulnerabilities and threat exposures are exploitable. In this way, we expedite prioritization and remediation, supporting strong CTEM (continuous threat exposure management) programs.  

Our work produces exploits, techniques, and tooling that are useful well beyond the assessment they came from. And when we find something worth sharing, this is where it lands. 

Think of this space as the workshop rather than the showroom. If you work in AppSec, AI security, or red teaming, or you’re curious about how AI systems and web applications fail, you’re in the right place.

The OWASP lists are a baseline. The agents construct attacks from what they find on the target, and that often leads outside them.

Testing runs on demand, inside CI/CD, or continuously as deployments change. We also retest a target to verify whether the original attack works against the applied remediation or mitigation measure.

Under the hood, we have specialized models we fine-tune for complex tasks, such as authorization reasoning, attack selection, target analysis, and adversarial AI testing. Around those sit a set of agents and a harness, with inference running on our own infrastructure.

## Questions we get asked

<img align="right" width="280" alt="Questions we get asked" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ed72618e-0404-417b-9b82-c4624fe58edc" />

- **Is Equixly open source?** </br>
The platform is closed. This organization is where we publish exploit proofs of concept, agent skills, tooling, and the research behind our write-ups.

- **How is Equixly different from a vulnerability scanner?** </br>
Vulnerability scanners typically identify potential weaknesses through predefined checks, payloads, and observed behavior. Equixly uses autonomous agents to reason about the target, construct attacks, and validate exploitability with proof-of-concept evidence.

- **Can I drive Equixly from my AI coding assistant?**
Yes. The [Equixly MCP Server](https://equixly.com/blog/2026/06/15/equixly-mcp-integration-continuous-security-testing-inside-your-ai-coding-assistant/) exposes the assessment loop to Claude, Copilot, GPT, and similar environments so that you can direct the agent in natural language. Equixly prepares the target, maps the API, selects probes, escalates weak signals, and organizes the evidence.

- **What is Polaris?** </br>
[Polaris](https://equixly.com/platform/polaris) is our in-platform AI assistant. It takes on the steps that usually slow a security workflow down (setting up services, defining authentication flows, configuring hooks for automation pipelines, and similar) and turns exploit findings into guidance engineering teams can act on.
